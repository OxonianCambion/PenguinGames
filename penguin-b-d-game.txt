Penguin 
penguin B - D game.

Set in antarctica - an icy background. Drawing, not a photo.
Penguins walk across the ice
It will show up on the screen "b" next, "d" second.
Each penguin has a "b" or "d" on its chest.
If you press the right button, it slides on its belly across the screen.
If wrong, the penguin falls into the water.

You get a point for each one you get right.
There are 10 penguins in a round. At the end, it shows the score. e.g. 5/10
The penguins go faster each round.
